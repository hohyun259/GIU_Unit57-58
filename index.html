Unit 57 · prefer and would rather
57A — prefer to / prefer -ing (일반적 선호)
57A
prefer to do 또는 prefer doing — 둘 다 가능
✓ I prefer to live in the country.
✓ I prefer living in the country.
57A
prefer -ing to -ing — to는 전치사 → 뒤에 반드시 동명사
✓ I prefer driving to taking the train.
✗ I prefer driving to take the train.
→ Unit 58C: 전치사 to 뒤에는 항상 -ing
57A
prefer -ing rather than (-ing)
✓ I prefer driving rather than taking the train.
57A
prefer to do rather than (do) — rather than 뒤 to 없이 원형
✓ I prefer to drive rather than take.
✗ I prefer to drive rather than to take.
57B — would prefer (특정 상황의 선호)
57B
would prefer to do — 동명사(-ing) 불가
✓ I'd prefer to drive.
✗ I'd prefer driving.
57B
I'd prefer to do rather than (do)
✓ I'd prefer to stay at home rather than go to the movies.
57C — would rather (I'd rather …)
57C
I'd rather + 동사원형 — to부정사 불가
✓ I'd rather drive.
✗ I'd rather to drive.
57C
부정: I'd rather not + 동사원형
✓ I'd rather not go out tonight.
57C
I'd rather do A than do B — than 뒤도 동사원형
✓ I'd rather stay at home than go to the movies.
✗ I'd rather stay than going.
57D — I'd rather somebody do something ⚠️ 시험 단골
57D
I'd rather + 사람 + 동사원형(subjunctive)
✓ I'd rather you drive.
✓ I'd rather you drove. (also past form)
✗ I'd rather you drives.
3인칭 단수형(-s)은 오답 — subjunctive = base form / past form 허용
Unit 58 · Preposition + -ing
58B — by -ing / before -ing / without -ing
58B
부정어 not은 전치사구 안에 위치: by not -ing
✓ She made herself sick by not eating well.
✗ She made herself sick not by eating well.
58C — 전치사 to + -ing
58C
to가 전치사일 때 뒤에 오는 동사는 반드시 -ing
✓ I prefer driving to taking the train.
✓ I'm looking forward to seeing you.
✗ I prefer driving to take the train.
✗ I'm looking forward to see you.
